About the UN SDGs

The 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), adopted by all United Nations Member States in 2015, provide a shared blueprint for peace and prosperity for people and the planet, now and into the future. At its heart are the 17 SDGs, which are an urgent call for action by all countries - developed and developing - in a global partnership.

1. No Poverty
2. Zero Hunger
3. Good Health
4. Quality Education
